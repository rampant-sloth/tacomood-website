📖 How to update dish names, descriptions, prices & publish live

Welcome to the Taco Mood menu editor! Here is how to keep your store up to date:

  1. Edit Dish Names: Click into the Dish Name field to rename any item.
  2. Edit Descriptions: Update ingredients, notes, dips, or preparation details in the Description field. Leave empty if no description is needed.
  3. Change Prices: Click directly into the Price field (e.g. £13.00) and enter the new price.
  4. Mark Sold Out: Check or uncheck "Mark Sold Out" next to any dish. Sold-out dishes will immediately show a bright orange badge on your live website.
  5. Publish Changes: Click the green "Publish Changes" button at the top. The updates are committed directly to your website repository in seconds!
