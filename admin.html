📖 How to add, hide or delete sections, edit dishes and publish live

Welcome to the Taco Mood menu editor! Each card on this page does one job. Make your changes, then press Publish Changes at the top.

✏️ Change a dish

  • Name: click into the Dish Name box and type the new name.
  • Description: update the Description box (ingredients, dips, notes). Leave it empty for no description.
  • Price: click into the Price box, e.g. £13.00.
  • Sold out: tick "Mark Sold Out" to keep the dish listed with an orange "Sold Out" badge on the website.

🙈 Hide something (nothing is deleted)

  • A dish: tick "Hide from website" on it; untick to bring it back.
  • A whole section: tick "Hide section" at the top of it; untick to bring it back.
  • Sold Out vs Hidden: "Sold Out" keeps the dish on the menu with a badge — use "Hide from website" if it should disappear completely.

➕ Add or 🗑️ remove

  • Add a section: press "➕ Add Section" above the menu, next to the search box. The name is the heading customers see, for example Sides or Desserts.
  • Add a dish: press "➕ Add Dish" at the top of the section, then fill in the name and price. Prices can be typed as 10, 10.5 or £10.00 — they are tidied up for you.
  • Delete a section: press "🗑️ Delete Section" and type the section name to confirm, so it cannot happen by accident. Press Undo in the green message if you change your mind.
  • Delete a dish: press "🗑️ Delete Dish" and type the dish name to confirm. Undo is offered straight afterwards.

🕒 Opening hours

  • In the Store Opening Hours card, tick "Closed" for shut days and set opening/closing times for open days. The website header and footer update automatically.

🔤 Wording at the top of the site

  • Use the "Website Text & Headings" card — the big title, the tagline, the menu heading and the sentence under it. Clear a box to hide that line, or press "↩️ Restore the original wording" to put it back.

🚀 Publish

  • Press the green "Publish Changes" button at the top. Your updates go straight to the live website — it usually refreshes within a minute.
