📖 How to update the menu & publish live

Welcome to the Taco Mood menu editor! Here is how to keep your store up to date:

  1. Change Prices: Click directly into any price text box (e.g. £13.00) and enter the new price.
  2. Sold Out: Check or uncheck "Mark as Sold Out" next to any dish. Sold-out dishes will immediately show a bright orange badge on your live website.
  3. Publish Changes: Click the green "Publish Changes" button at the top. You will be prompted for your Admin Key (GitHub Fine-Grained Personal Access Token with Contents: Read and write permission). The changes are committed directly to your website in seconds!
